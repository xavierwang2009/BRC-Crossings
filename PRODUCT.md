## Platform

## Stack

Now: a single self-contained HTML file (the clickable prototype), loading libraries from a CDN. Next step, confirmed by the user: port to a React web app, so state, screens and the Companion renderer should stay cleanly separable.

## Users

Adults (18+) in and around the Bridge River Counseling community in Dallas and Frisco, Texas, many from Chinese-speaking immigrant families. They come as parents (including parents of kids with ADHD), couples and people preparing for marriage, people under work stress, and people dealing with anxiety, low mood or family strain. They read English, Traditional Chinese or Simplified Chinese. They open the app in small private moments (evenings, after the kids are asleep, a break at work) wanting to be heard without being exposed. (From the Phase 1 prototype; not separately confirmed.)

## Product Purpose

BRC Crossings (同渡) is a community app by Bridge River Counseling. It gives Members a safe place to share and be heard: a peer feed and Circles, questions answered by licensed Counselors as general guidance, a Counselor "guide" they can message, a daily mood check-in with a Companion pet, and a starter library written by Counselors. Success is Members returning, feeling supported, finding helpful answers, and choosing a 1:1 session on the clinic website when they need more.

## Positioning

A clinic-run, bilingual (English / 繁 / 简) peer community where licensed Counselors answer in the open and a Counselor guide is one tap away, inside a warm daily ritual with a Companion that never punishes absence.

## Operating Context

- Phase 1 screens: onboarding (age and consent, language, Focus Areas with a main one, location, Companion), Feed, Ask/compose (Named or Anonymous per post), thread, Counselor guide matching and DM with a booking card, Companion check-in, RiverSeeds shop and badges, Circles, Learn, Me, and the crisis sheet.
- Booking happens on bridgerivercounseling.com; DMs are never sent to clinic records.
- Phase 2 (not built): Circle Stewards and Counselor drop-ins.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Not therapy and not an emergency service; this is stated wherever Counselors speak.
- Counselor 1:1 services are gated by the Member's stated location and the Counselor's state licenses (TX, WA, AR, VA in the sample). Out-of-state and international Members get the community plus a referral list.
- Anonymous posting: no one, including Counselors, can see the author; clinic safety officers can in an emergency.
- Risky posts are held for review and crisis help is shown immediately.
- RiverSeeds are earned (daily cap), cannot be bought or cashed out, balances are private, no leaderboards.
- The Companion naps if a day is skipped; it never gets sick.
- Chinese posts convert between Traditional and Simplified automatically; machine translation is labelled.
- Open: the Chinese-language crisis line partner is not confirmed yet.

## Product Principles

1. Safety before engagement: help is always one tap away, and nothing rewards distress or volume.
2. The Member controls exposure: identity, mood sharing and Focus Areas are private unless they choose otherwise.
3. Counselors guide, they don't treat: every Counselor touchpoint says what it is and isn't, and points to booking for real care.
4. Equal in three languages: every screen works fully in English, 繁體 and 简体.
5. Warmth without guilt: the Companion comforts and celebrates; absence never costs anything.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Adults of all ages, including older parents and grandparents reading Chinese. Large readable type in both scripts, generous touch targets, reduced-motion support for all animation, and screen-reader labels in the active language.
